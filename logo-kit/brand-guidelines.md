# KERN — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo

**Idea:** A calibrated K with a measured point at its centre: strength begins with knowing where to start.

**Versions:** horizontal · stacked · symbol-only · wordmark-only · small-size symbol.

Use the horizontal lockup as the default. Use the stacked lockup in square spaces. Use the symbol alone where the KERN name is already visible.

## 2. Clear space

Keep clear space on every side equal to **four times the diameter of the lime point**. In the master files that is **56 units**: about 7.6% of the horizontal lockup width. No type, border, photograph, or other mark may enter this zone.

The point stays the unit of measure even when it is not visible at the size in use.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---:|---:|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 72 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 24 px wide; 16 px with the small-size file | 6 mm wide |

Below 24 px, use `kern-symbol-small.svg` or the one-colour mark. At 16 px, use the favicon export rather than scaling the lockup.

## 4. Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK approximation | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1b1d19` | 27 29 25 | 7 0 14 89 | Primary mark, type |
| Bone | `#f3f0e8` | 243 240 232 | 0 1 5 5 | Light background, reversed mark |
| Moss | `#4a5545` | 74 85 69 | 13 0 19 67 | Dark field, optional mono mark |
| Signal lime | `#d8f36b` | 216 243 107 | 11 0 56 5 | Measure point only |

Approved pairs: ink mark on bone/paper · bone mark on ink or moss · moss mark on bone/paper · one-colour mark where reproduction is limited.

Never use lime as text on bone, never use lime as the body of the mark, and never place the mark on a mid-tone or busy image.

## 5. The point

The lime point sits in the open notch of the K. It reads clearly on ink and moss. On bone it is a quiet accent: do not rely on it for recognition, and do not enlarge it or add a ring behind it to compensate.

At 16 px the point is below one pixel and effectively disappears. That is expected. The mark must still read without it.

## 6. Typography

The wider identity uses Fraunces for display headings and Manrope for body and UI. The logo itself is custom outlined geometry. Do not recreate the wordmark by typing `KERN` in Fraunces, Manrope, or another font.

## 7. Do

- Keep the mark flat, quiet, and sharply aligned.
- Use the correct horizontal or stacked lockup for the available space.
- Use the one-colour version for embroidery, stamps, and constrained print.
- Place the logo on calm areas of photography.
- Scale from the SVG masters, never from a screenshot.

## 8. Don't

Do not stretch, squash, rotate, outline, shadow, bevel, gradient-fill, or recolour the mark outside the KERN palette. Do not rearrange the symbol and wordmark. Do not move the point, enlarge it, or use it as a decorative pattern. Do not use an all-lime symbol on an ink tile: the point merges with the arms.

## 9. Handover

The SVG masters and generated variants are in this folder. The presentation and test sheet use the real exports, not redrawn copies. A professional trademark search is still recommended before using KERN as a live business identity; this concept project does not include trademark clearance, and it shows no real contact details, staff names, or address.
