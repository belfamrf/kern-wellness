KERN — identity01 / 07

01 / Brief

A quiet room, measured work.

KERN is a strength and mobility studio for busy adults who want a clear starting point, not a louder gym and not a clinical treatment.

Calm

Bone walls, one small signal of action, and time to pay attention.

Precise

Assessment before training. Numbers treated as measurements, not decoration.

Human

Small groups, plain language, no performance theatre.

The logo had to feel like a measurement taken calmly, not a badge earned.

KERN — identity02 / 07

02 / The mark

A K built on a datum.

  • 01One vertical datum.The stem is the measure line. Every curve starts from it.
  • 02Two measured diagonals.Each arm mirrors the other, so the mark reads as built rather than drawn.
  • 03One point.A lime bead sits in the open notch, where an assessment would begin.

Construction diagram, not to scale. The point is a detail of the mark, not a recognition device: it is visible on ink and moss, and stays quiet on bone.

06 / Handover

What is in the kit.

masters/Five outlined SVGs: symbol, small symbol, wordmark, horizontal, stacked.
exports/Ink, bone, and one-colour versions as SVG and PNG, plus favicons, app icons, and a web manifest.
brand-guidelines.mdClear space, minimum sizes, colour pairs, and usage rules.
kern-preview.htmlTest sheet for small sizes, reversed artwork, and misuse.

Before launch. Run a trademark search for KERN in the relevant class. Replace the concept-project label wherever the mark is shown in public.

Check in place. Look at the 16 px favicon and the 120 px header on a real screen, light and dark, before signing off.

Do not rebuild. The wordmark is outlined geometry. Type it in Fraunces or Manrope and it is no longer the logo.
